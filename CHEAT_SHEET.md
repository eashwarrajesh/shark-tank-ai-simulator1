# 🦈 Shark Tank AI - Cheat Sheet

## 🎯 Quick Reference for Strong Answers

---

## 📋 The "Number-Name-Evidence" Formula

Every strong answer needs:
1. **Number**: Specific metric (₹500, 10 customers, 3 months)
2. **Name**: Specific example (IIT Delhi, Sarah from pilot, Zomato)
3. **Evidence**: Proof (pilot results, survey, test campaign)

---

## 💰 Money Shark Questions → How to Answer

| Question Type | Bad Answer | Good Answer |
|--------------|------------|-------------|
| **Revenue Model** | "We'll charge a fee" | "15% commission per transaction. Average ₹500/session = ₹75 revenue per booking" |
| **Break-even** | "Soon, once we scale" | "₹50k monthly costs / ₹75 per session = 667 sessions needed = 170 active users (3mo target)" |
| **Pricing** | "Competitive pricing" | "₹199/month. Competitors charge ₹299-499. We surveyed 50 students, 38 said ₹199 is fair" |

**Money Shark cares about**: Revenue, costs, margins, timeline to profit

---

## 🦈 Market Shark Questions → How to Answer

| Question Type | Bad Answer | Good Answer |
|--------------|------------|-------------|
| **Customer** | "College students" | "3rd-year engineering students at IIT Delhi who struggle with CS assignments before exams" |
| **Why Switch** | "Better product" | "Current solution (WhatsApp groups) takes 2+ days for response. We guarantee 10 minutes" |
| **First 10 Customers** | "Social media marketing" | "8 students from my hostel committed. 5 more from CS Facebook group test post (47 comments)" |

**Market Shark cares about**: Specific people, switching costs, acquisition plan

---

## 🚀 Product Shark Questions → How to Answer

| Question Type | Bad Answer | Good Answer |
|--------------|------------|-------------|
| **Competition** | "No one else does this" | "Vedantu/Chegg take 2-3 days. We match in 10min. Big platforms can't copy our hyperlocal pool" |
| **Risk** | "We'll handle it" | "Bad transaction = instant refund + tutor review. 3 strikes = permanent ban from platform" |
| **Team** | "Smart and motivated" | "Built 2 student apps before (500+ users). CS background. 15 tutor interviews done" |

**Product Shark cares about**: Defensibility, risks, team capability

---

## 🎯 Score Boosters

### ✅ Always Include:
- **Specific numbers**: ₹500, 15%, 10 customers, month 3
- **Timelines**: "week 1", "by end of Q1", "in next 30 days"
- **Named examples**: "IIT Delhi", "Sarah from pilot", "Instagram test"
- **Evidence keywords**: "tested", "surveyed", "piloted", "based on"
- **Connectors**: "because", "specifically", "for example"

### ❌ Avoid:
- "Huge market"
- "Everyone needs this"
- "We'll go viral"
- "Once we scale"
- "We'll figure it out"
- "Better UX"

---

## 📊 Scoring Rubric

| Score | What It Means | Example |
|-------|---------------|---------|
| **0-40** | Vague, no specifics | "We'll do marketing and grow" |
| **41-60** | Some detail, missing evidence | "We'll target college students through social media" |
| **61-75** | Good specifics, minor gaps | "Targeting IIT Delhi 3rd years via FB groups. 500+ members" |
| **76-90** | Strong answer, concrete plan | "8 pre-committed from hostel + FB group post got 47 interested = 10 customer target in week 1-2" |
| **91-100** | Exceptional (rare) | Above + pilot data + retention rate + CAC calculation |

## 💡 Hint System Guide

Each investor gives domain-specific hints:

| Investor | Hint Style |
|----------|-----------|
| **Money Shark** | Points to revenue/cost numbers to calculate |
| **Market Shark** | Suggests naming real people or test groups |
| **Product Shark** | Hints at defensible advantages (time, trust, data) |

**Pro tip**: Use hints to understand what evidence type to include

## 🎓 Common Question Patterns

### Money Questions:
- "What is your revenue model?"
- "How long to break even?"
- "What stops users from bypassing your platform?"

### Market Questions:
- "Name one specific person who would pay"
- "Why would they switch?"
- "How will you get first 10 customers?"

### Product Questions:
- "What prevents Google/Facebook from copying?"
- "What's your biggest risk?"
- "Why is this team uniquely qualified?"

## 📈 Answer Quality Examples

### Revenue Question:

**Score 45** ❌
> "We make money from commissions. The market is huge so revenue will grow fast."

**Score 75** ✅
> "15% commission per session. Avg session ₹500 = ₹75 revenue. Need 667 sessions/month for ₹50k break-even. Based on 50-student pilot, 2 sessions/week per user = 170 active students needed."

---

### Customer Question:

**Score 40** ❌
> "College students who need tutoring help."

**Score 80** ✅
> "Rohan, 3rd year CS at IIT Delhi, pays ₹800/month for last-minute help. Surveyed 30 students like him, 23 said they'd switch from WhatsApp groups because of guaranteed 10min response."

---

### Competition Question:

**Score 50** ❌
> "We have better tech and first-mover advantage."

**Score 78** ✅
> "Vedantu takes 2-3 days to match (national pool). We match in 10 minutes (hyperlocal). Big platforms can't replicate because: (1) Need to rebuild matching for real-time, (2) Their tutors are spread nationally, (3) We're building city-by-city density. 200 Bangalore tutors already vetted."

## 🚨 Red Flags to Avoid

| Red Flag | Why It's Bad | Fix |
|----------|--------------|-----|
| "Viral growth" | Unprovable hope | "Referral rate 3:1 in pilot over 2 weeks" |
| "No competitors" | Shows no research | "Zomato/Swiggy exist but 25% fee vs our 8%" |
| "Huge TAM" | Too vague | "2.5M engineering students in tier-1 cities" |
| "We'll pivot" | Lacks conviction | "Focused on tutoring, validated with 50 interviews" |

## 🔄 Practice Loop

1. Run pitch with **vague answers** → See low scores
2. Run same pitch with **specific answers** → See score jump
3. Compare feedback on both runs
4. Learn what "specific" actually means

---

## 🎁 Pro Tips

1. **Open with numbers**: "₹75 per session" not "small commission"
2. **Middle with names**: "Tested with 15 IIT Delhi students" not "students"
3. **Close with timeline**: "Week 1-2 target" not "soon"
4. **Use "because"**: Forces you to add reasoning
5. **Reference pilot/survey**: Instant credibility boost
